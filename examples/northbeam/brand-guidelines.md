# Brand Guidelines: Northbeam Coffee

Northbeam is a small-batch coffee roaster by the harbor. The brand feels warm,
crafted, and calm: more neighborhood roaster than tech startup.

## Colors

Use only these colors, written exactly as these hex values. The other skills
refer to them by **role**.

| Role | Hex | Name | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| primary | `#1F3A2E` | Forest | Headlines, large color blocks, and the background of bold designs |
| accent | `#E8B04B` | Honey | One highlight per design: a number, a rule, a badge |
| background | `#FAF6EF` | Cream | The default canvas |
| surface | `#FFFFFF` | White | Cards on top of the canvas, and text on forest |
| text | `#1A1A1A` | Ink | Body text |
| muted | `#5E6B63` | Sage | Captions, sources, and small labels |

**Pairings that pass contrast:** ink or forest on cream, and white or honey on
forest. Honey on cream fails, so never use honey for text on the cream canvas.

## Type

| Role | Font | Weights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| heading | Fraunces | 600 or 700, with tight line spacing |
| body | Inter | 400 to 600 |

- At most two sizes of headline and two sizes of body text per design.
- Minimum text size: 24px on social cards and squares, 32px on stories and slides.

## Layout

- One focal point per design. If everything is bold, nothing is.
- Left-aligned text reads as more crafted than centered text.
- Leave at least a third of the canvas empty.

## Voice

Warm, plain, a little playful. Short sentences. No exclamation marks in headlines.
Say "you", not "customers".

## Never

- Off-brand colors or fonts, even if someone asks. Offer the closest brand option.
- More than one accent color moment per design.
- Gradients, drop shadows, or clip-art icons.
