# Social Card

A social card has one job: get one message across in the second someone scrolls
past it. Default format: `social-card` (1200x630). Use `square` for Instagram and
`story` for stories.

Colors and fonts below are **roles**. Look up the actual hex values and font
names in `brand-guidelines`.

## Layout

Use the **primary** color as the background, and the brand's light colors for text.

1. **Eyebrow** (optional): a short label above the headline, like "NEW THIS WEEK". Body font, weight 600, 24 to 28px, written in uppercase letters, with letter-spacing 2, in the **accent** color.
2. **Headline:** the message, in the user's exact words. Heading font, weight 700, 64 to 88px, in **surface** or **background**, at most three lines.
3. **Support line** (optional): one sentence. Body font, weight 400, 28 to 32px, in **surface** or **background**.
4. **Sign-off:** the brand name, body font, weight 600, 24px, in the bottom-right corner.

Keep a 72px margin on every side. Left-align the eyebrow, headline, and support
line, and center them vertically as a group.

## Checks before you call it done

- The headline is the user's exact copy, and it fits on at most three lines.
- Nothing is closer than 72px to an edge.
- There is exactly one accent moment.
